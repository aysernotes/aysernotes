whatever comes to your mind just write it. but to refine what you write, you need to read.
let me explain in simple words: suppose you would like to take a gym membership and you do receive the membership. despite the membership, a main hurdle comes along the way. you need to have the proteins, the mass, the built flesh inside your body to get the possibility to atleast hold a two kg dumble in your  hand. so what you? you take right amount of food, with right nutrients, create a roadmap and much more.

but your brain has a different function. for the same amount of output, you require the same amount of input. why am i saying this? because let me tell you something, when you sit to write, you  are expressing: the ideas, the thoughts, the process, the pain, the feelings, all sorts of human characters. but you need to know what you are expressing, you need to have the jest of idea what you are trying to explain, express, or affirm. see, writing helps you excruciate your pain. but you need to read to completely comprehend  what you are trying to express and that cant happen until you just read.

### [[conditions]] you need to read in:

but before we start to read, reading requires excess amount of power:  the power to focus and what you eat decides the input that later can be used for the output.

as for the focus power, you need a well lit room(a candle can be enough), a room that is silent(probably outskirts of your home if people are not running errands at your house). i know this seems to be a bit of sensless blabber, but an environment perfectly reflects the house of your brain that helps you grasp concepts, ideas, reflections, emotions, anything that can be an imaginary world. the commotion in the scenes, the emotions between the characters, the true word of god, this requires light. just as you save the candle light from the wind you cover it using the glass lamp around it, you need an environment to garner the light strenght inside you. this will teach you patience, ability to comprehend tasks, tolerate different opinions and most importantly express your thoughts,
