# Hi, I'm Ayser.

I'm an Electronics and Communication Engineering graduate exploring the intersection of hardware and software. My work revolves around embedded systems, firmware development, hardware design, and building practical solutions that solve real-world problems.

I enjoy understanding how things work from the lowest level up - whether that's writing Embedded C for a microcontroller, designing a circuit, developing a web application with Django, or automating workflows with Python. I'm currently experimenting with platforms like the STM32F446RE and Arduino Uno R3 while continuing to expand my knowledge of modern embedded systems.

This website is my digital workshop.

Here you'll find projects I've built, articles documenting what I've learned, tutorials that break down complex topics into approachable explanations, and occasional thoughts on technology, society, politics, and everyday life. Some posts will be deeply technical. Others will simply capture ideas worth thinking about.

## Why this website exists

The internet has no shortage of polished success stories, but far fewer places that honestly document the journey of learning and building.

This site is my attempt to create that kind of space - a place where experiments, failures, improvements, and completed work all have equal value. Whether you're an engineer, a student, a hobbyist, or simply curious about technology, I hope you find something here that's useful or thought-provoking.

If you'd like to collaborate, discuss an idea, or simply say hello, feel free to get in touch.
